One precise question at a time

Judge the extraction against the article, not against plausibility.

  1. Read the exact question and the candidate value.
  2. Read as much of the complete paper as your judgment requires.
  3. Mark the text passages that were important to your answer; these teach us where field evidence occurs. This is the most important step in the whole process: your marked passages verify what the strongest evidence in the article is, and in problematic cases they are what the system learns from.
  4. Correct the value in your own words when it is wrong or imprecise.

This pilot builds a repair-learning set. It does not certify corpus accuracy.
